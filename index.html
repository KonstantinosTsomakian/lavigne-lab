Why does RNA Pol II hit pause?

Hi, we're the Lavigne Lab! We're curious about how cells switch their genes on, how they keep their DNA in one piece when things get stressful, and how all of that decides what a cell becomes. We mix wet-lab experiments with a lot of sequencing and code.

📄 See our papers 👋 Meet the people

What we're curious about

Three big questions keep us busy (and occasionally up at night).

Go, stop, go again

How RNA polymerase II starts, pauses and takes off along genes, both protein-coding ones and the non-coding kind (lncRNAs, enhancer RNAs).

Keeping DNA in one piece

What cells do to protect their genome when the environment or their own genetics put it under stress, and how transcription and DNA repair talk to each other.

Networks that pick a fate

How gene regulatory networks steer a cell toward what it becomes, in normal development and when things go wrong in disease.
